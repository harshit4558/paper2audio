## Weak Areas — Always Explain

**Differential equations:** ODEs, initial conditions, first/second-order systems, numerical solutions, stability

**Probability & statistics:** distributions, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, expectation/variance/covariance, likelihood, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, regression

**Optimisation & information theory:** gradient descent, convexity, convergence, regularisation, entropy, KL divergence

Having used Python and AI coding tools ≠ mastery. Explain:

## Style Notes

- Explain from first principles before equations
- Use real-world analogies and concrete examples
